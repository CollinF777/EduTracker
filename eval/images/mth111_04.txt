Linear Equations
Slope-intercept form
Formula: y = mx + b
Definition: Slope-intercept is a way to write a linear
equation that makes it easy to identify the slope and
y-intercept
m: slope
b: y-intercept
x: independent variable
y: dependent variable
Example: m=3, b=-2
y = mx + b → y = 3x - 2
m = (y2-y1)/(x2-x1) → m = (7-3)/(4-2) = 4/2 = 2
Key point: Slope tells you how steep a line is,
while the y-intercept tells you where it crosses
the y-axis
