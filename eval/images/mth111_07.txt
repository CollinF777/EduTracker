Systems of Linear Equations and Inequalities
A system of equations consists of two or more
equations that share variables. The solution is the
point that satisfies every equation
Example: Solve the system: 2x + y = 7
3x - y = 8
(2x+y) + (3x-y) = 7+8
5x = 15 → x = 3
2(3) + y = 7
6 + y = 7
y = 1
(3,1)
Key concepts:
Elimination works by adding or subtracting
equations to cancel one variable
