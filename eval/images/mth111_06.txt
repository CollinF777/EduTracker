Exponents and Polynomials
Topic: Properties of Exponents
Definition: Exponents represent repeated multiplication.
Exponents rules make it easier to simplify algebraic
expressions.
Important Rules:
Rule Formula
Product x^a x^b = x^(a+b)
Quotient x^a / x^b = x^(a-b)
Power (x^a)^b = x^(ab)
Zero x^0 = 1
Negative x^(-a) = 1 / x^a
Example: ((x^3)^2 · x^4) / x^5 → (x^6 · x^4) / x^5 → x^10 / x^5 = x^5
Key Concepts: When multiplying like bases, add
exponents. When dividing like bases, subtract
exponents
