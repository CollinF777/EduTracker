Linear Equations and Inequalities (1 variable)
Topic: Solving Multi-Step Linear Equations
Definition: A linear equation is an equation where the variable
has an exponent of 1. The goal is to isolate the
variable on one side.
Important Rules:
Distribute before combining like terms
Combine like terms
Use inverse operations to isolate the variable
Whatever operation you preform on one side must
also be preformed on the other
Example:
Solve: 3(2x-4)+5=17
Step 1: Distribute.
6x-12+5=17
Step 2: Combine like terms
6x-7=17
Step 3: Add 7 to both sides
6x=24
Step 4: Divide by 6
x=4
Key Takeaway: Always isolate the variable
using inverse operations
